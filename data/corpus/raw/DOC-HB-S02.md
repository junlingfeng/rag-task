# Handbook Supplement - Beijing Branch

## Roles and Responsibilities

Beijing Branch implementation: Employees submit accurate applications and supporting documents; direct managers review business rationale and roster impact; department heads coordinate staffing; HR interprets policy and makes final determinations. This supplement is effective from version 2021.

## Process and Systems

Beijing Branch implementation: All processes in this chapter are initiated in the HR system, with an approval chain of direct manager, department head and HR. If the system is unavailable, requests may be logged with the HR service desk and are handled within 2 working days. This supplement is effective from version 2021.

## Records and Archiving

Beijing Branch implementation: All approval records are archived automatically by the HR system for the duration of the employment relationship. Employees may view their own history through the self-service portal; third parties have no access. This supplement is effective from version 2021.
