# Handbook Supplement - Tokyo Representative Office

## Definitions

Tokyo Representative Office implementation: In this chapter, 'working day' means a statutory working day excluding public holidays and weekends, and 'direct manager' means the direct reporting line, excluding project-based dotted-line relationships. This supplement is effective from version 2025.

## Scope

Tokyo Representative Office implementation: This chapter applies to all regular employees, probationary employees and dispatched staff. Arrangements for interns and external consultants are agreed separately by the hiring department and HR and are not fully covered here. This supplement is effective from version 2025.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Tokyo Representative Office implementation: Employees submit accurate applications and supporting documents; direct managers review business rationale and roster impact; department heads coordinate staffing; HR interprets policy and makes final determinations. This supplement is effective from version 2025.
