# Compliance Supplement - Tokyo Representative Office

## Policy Review

Tokyo Representative Office implementation: The Compliance function reviews these policies annually and publishes updates when regulations change. Employees are required to read the latest version and acknowledge it in the system. This supplement is effective from version 2025.

## Scope

Tokyo Representative Office implementation: This guide applies to all directors, managers and employees, as well as third-party agents, consultants and suppliers acting on behalf of the company. Violations by third parties may lead to contract termination. This supplement is effective from version 2025.

## Compliance Responsibilities

Tokyo Representative Office implementation: Business leaders hold first-line responsibility for compliance outcomes in their units; Compliance sets policy, delivers training and conducts investigations; Internal Audit independently assesses control effectiveness. This supplement is effective from version 2025.
