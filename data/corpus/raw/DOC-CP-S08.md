# Compliance Supplement - New York Office

## Compliance Responsibilities

New York Office implementation: Business leaders hold first-line responsibility for compliance outcomes in their units; Compliance sets policy, delivers training and conducts investigations; Internal Audit independently assesses control effectiveness. This supplement is effective from version 2027.

## Training and Communication

New York Office implementation: New hires complete foundational compliance training after onboarding; existing staff attend annual refresher sessions. Policy updates are communicated by email and the intranet. This supplement is effective from version 2027.

## Records and Evidence

New York Office implementation: Compliance approvals, declarations and investigation records are kept in the compliance system; business units must not delete or alter them. Investigations follow confidentiality principles. This supplement is effective from version 2027.
