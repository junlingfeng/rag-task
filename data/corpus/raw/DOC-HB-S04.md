# Handbook Supplement - Singapore Office

## Records and Archiving

Singapore Office implementation: All approval records are archived automatically by the HR system for the duration of the employment relationship. Employees may view their own history through the self-service portal; third parties have no access. This supplement is effective from version 2023.

## Violations and Appeals

Singapore Office implementation: Violations may result in a reminder, written warning or termination depending on severity. Employees who disagree with an outcome may appeal to HR after receiving notice. This supplement is effective from version 2023.

## Definitions

Singapore Office implementation: In this chapter, 'working day' means a statutory working day excluding public holidays and weekends, and 'direct manager' means the direct reporting line, excluding project-based dotted-line relationships. This supplement is effective from version 2023.
