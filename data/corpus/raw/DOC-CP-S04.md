# Compliance Supplement - Singapore Office

## Records and Evidence

Singapore Office implementation: Compliance approvals, declarations and investigation records are kept in the compliance system; business units must not delete or alter them. Investigations follow confidentiality principles. This supplement is effective from version 2023.

## Consequences

Singapore Office implementation: Employees violating compliance policy may face disciplinary action, termination, recovery of losses or referral to authorities. Managers who fail to supervise may be held accountable as well. This supplement is effective from version 2023.

## Policy Review

Singapore Office implementation: The Compliance function reviews these policies annually and publishes updates when regulations change. Employees are required to read the latest version and acknowledge it in the system. This supplement is effective from version 2023.
